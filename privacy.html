A NOTE ON PRIVACY

Simple website.
Clear information.

Here’s how information is handled when you browse this portfolio or get in touch.

Last updated · 13 September 2026

At a glance

No visitor accounts, payment forms or embedded contact forms.

No analytics or advertising trackers added by this portfolio.

Ask a privacy question ↗
01 / BROWSING

Visiting the website

You can browse without signing in or submitting personal details. The hosting provider may process technical information such as your IP address, requested pages and request times to serve and operate the website.

02 / GETTING IN TOUCH

Email and the clipboard

Email links open your chosen email application. If you send a message, your email address and its contents are used to respond and continue the conversation. Please avoid sending sensitive information that is not needed for your enquiry.

The “Copy email address” button writes my public email address to your clipboard only when you select it. It does not read your clipboard.

03 / ON YOUR DEVICE

Preferences and offline files

Your light or dark appearance choice is saved in your browser’s local storage. A service worker also caches website files to support repeat visits and offline fallback pages. These features do not create a visitor account or advertising profile.

You can remove these stored files and preferences by clearing this website’s site data in your browser settings.

04 / OTHER SERVICES

Fonts, icons and external links

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts and icons from the cdnjs content delivery network. Your browser makes requests to those services, which can receive technical details such as your IP address and browser information.

Links to the school website, LinkedIn and Instagram open external services. Their own privacy practices apply when you visit them. Email is handled by the email providers used for the conversation.

05 / QUESTIONS & UPDATES

Get in touch

For questions about information you have shared, or to request its deletion, email aprojeetbhowmick2008@gmail.com.
