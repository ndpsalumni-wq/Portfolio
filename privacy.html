Policy

Your privacy, explained clearly.

This page explains how information may be handled when you visit or contact me through this portfolio.

Last updated: 4 August 2026

Information collected

This portfolio does not contain an account system, analytics dashboard, advertising tracker, payment facility or embedded contact form. I do not intentionally collect personal information merely because you browse the website.

Email communication

When you select an email link, your chosen email application opens. Any information you send is provided voluntarily and is used only to understand and respond to your message.

Technical logs

The website's hosting provider may automatically process limited technical data, such as an IP address, browser type, requested page, date and time, for security, reliability and server operation. Such processing is controlled by the hosting provider.

External websites

The portfolio links to websites and services such as LinkedIn, GitHub, Instagram, New Delhi Public School and email providers. Their own privacy policies and terms apply after you leave this website.

Cookies and local preferences

The portfolio may store a small local preference in your browser, such as your selected light or dark appearance. This remains on your device and is not used to identify you.

Project information

Project descriptions are presented to demonstrate design, development and product capabilities. Private school records, credentials, confidential source code and personal student information are not intentionally published in this portfolio.
